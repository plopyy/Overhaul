# Changelog

## 2.5.0 (in progress)

## 2.4.0 (in progress)

### Added
- **Grimoire stand**, a new hammer piece (Crafting, at a workbench: 8 fine wood, 4 bronze, 1 surtling core): a candlelit book stand and its tall candle holder in the game’s own wood wall material, wearing, weathering and lighting like any wooden wall (the grain running along their posts), with candles burning with their own flames (no black wicks) and a soft, warm yellow light that flickers. Its grimoire is closed, plays its opening animation as soon as any player comes near (within 4 m) and its closing animation when nobody is left around; every player sees it. As the book opens, the three candles of the tall holder rise about 15 cm off it, one after the other, and float with a gentle up-and-down sway as if stirred by the wind (each at its own rhythm, in a seamless loop) while a player stays near; as the book closes they settle back exactly where they were. Using it opens the grimoire window, which closes if you walk away from the stand. It can be moved with the move key like the other functional pieces. Its build menu icon shows the stand itself. (2.4.0.105)
- **Eitr from food**, set in the new **Foods.cfg** (sent by the server): every food without eitr of its own now gives 15 % of its health + stamina as eitr (carrot soup: 9, bread: 14), and four new **eitr dishes** at the cauldron lead up to the Mistlands’ eitr dishes (stuffed mushroom: 75 eitr): **Yellow mushroom stew** (Black Forest: yellow mushrooms, mushrooms, thistle; 15 eitr), **Turnip and mushroom soup** (Swamp: turnips, yellow mushrooms, bloodbag; 30 eitr), **Onion infusion** (Mountains: onions, mushrooms, honey; 45 eitr) and **Cloud flatbread** (Plains: barley flour, cloudberries, yellow mushrooms; 60 eitr), each with low health and stamina and an eitr-blue icon.
- **Overhaul staffs**, one per biome, new items next to the game’s staffs (left unchanged), each crafted from a wood, the biome’s metal and a biome ingredient: **Sylvan staff** (Black Forest, forge: wood, bronze, surtling cores), **Spectral staff** (Swamp, forge: ancient bark, iron, ectoplasm), **Runic staff** (Mountains, forge: core wood, silver, freeze glands), **Totemic staff** (Plains, forge: fine wood, black metal, fuling totems), **Mist staff** (Mistlands, galdr table: Yggdrasil wood, black metal, black cores) and **Ash staff** (Ashlands, galdr table: blackwood, flametal, molten cores). The Totemic staff has its own model (wood with its own texture, black metal rings, a fuling totem with its purple glow and sparks) and its own icon, drawn by the game from the model like the game’s staff icons (head in close-up, staff on a diagonal, its head glow included); the **Spectral staff** takes the staff of fracturing’s model with its smooth crystals and gems turned from red to the ectoplasm’s pale aqua green, and the fuling totem’s glow and sparks of the Totemic staff around its head, in that same colour (and its own icon too); the **Mist staff** has its own model too, the staff of embers’ wood in a darker, warmer brown, with black metal bands and a black core hanging under its crook, with the fuling totem’s purple glow and sparks around the core (and its own icon); the Ash staff uses the staff of embers’ model until its own is made. Each has its own blocking shield and follows the rarity system. Staffs no longer have blunt or elemental damage but a new base stat, **Magic power** (20 for the Sylvan staff up to 100 for the Ash staff, raised by each upgrade level and by rarity), shown in the tooltip; magic power has no element: each spell deals a percent of it as its own element’s damage (a 120 % fire spell with 100 power = 120 fire damage), shown in the grimoire; until the grimoire spells are cast, the staffs fire their base staff’s spell at 100 % (fireball: fire, ice shards: frost). New **Magic power** effect in Effects.cfg, flat, added to the equipped staff’s magic power: a gear enchantment for now, and the one effect class skills and buffs will use too. Magic power is the staff’s own damage stat, computed like any weapon damage (upgrade levels, rarity, damage effects, skill), and only that part of the hit is turned into the spell’s element when the spell hits (a weapon’s other damage, blunt, lightning…, is dealt as usual); in the tooltip it is the Magic power line next to the other damage lines. **Magic** is a damage category like Physical: magic power, fire, frost and lightning. The game’s generic damage is now magic power on every weapon: it only serves spells and is never dealt by a hit without a spell. New effects: **Magic damage** (%, staff enchantment, raises magic power, fire, frost and lightning damage) and **Magic resistance** (%, armour and shield enchantment, less fire, frost and lightning damage taken). Each staff holds 4 spell slots (2 normal spells, 1 special, 1 shield): the grimoire now installs spells in the equipped staff, and each staff keeps its own.
- **First spells**, known from the start and listed in the new **Spells.cfg** (sent by the server; name, element, type, tier, unlock level, damage in % of the magic power, eitr cost, cast attack and projectile, projectile size, speed and gravity, slow and stun values): **Fireball** (fire, 100 %, 5 eitr: Overhaul’s straight fireball, 50 % faster, exploding on impact and setting enemies on fire), **Ice shard** (ice, 100 %, 5 eitr: one ice shard twice as big, particles and glow included, 50 % faster, flying straight at the crosshair without falling, slowing the movement and attacks of the creatures hit by 50 % for 4 s) and **Lightning** (lightning, 100 %, 5 eitr: Overhaul’s lightning falling where you aim, 35 % chance to stun each creature hit for 2 s: no move, no attack). Each has its own icon (a fireball, an ice shard, a lightning bolt), and the grimoire's element tabs and page header have their own emblems (a flame, an ice crystal, a bolt) instead of the game's icons; each element has its colour: orange-red fire, blue ice, yellow lightning. Bookmark icons keep the same gap to the bookmark's end whether it is open or not, and the installed spell slots are named Normal, Normal, Special and Shield, an empty slot shows no text. The grimoire columns are tiers (Tier I to III); the rank pips under a spell are the spell’s own rank. An Overhaul staff casts the spell installed in its first slot with the light attack and the one of its second slot with the heavy attack; with no spell installed it does not attack.
- **Grimoire** (spell book), first version, opened with F1 for now (Settings > Overhaul > Shortcuts): an open book in the Auga look. On the right page, one bookmark per element (fire, ice, lightning) and the element's spells by type (normal, special, shield) and rank, ranks II and III showing the class level that unlocks them. On the left page, the details of the clicked spell (eitr, damage, cast time, area) and the 4 installed spells (2 normal spells, 1 special, 1 shield). Installing a spell: drag and drop it onto a matching slot. The spells are those of Spells.cfg (the demo list is gone), empty cards are hidden; the installed spells are those of the equipped staff.

### Fixed
- Items dropped by tamed creatures (at death or when their corpse vanishes) no longer disappear from the ground after 30 minutes. (2.4.0.244)
- Grimoire stand: it could show the pack’s own wood instead of the game’s wood wall planks; its wood is now the wood wall’s material itself, set in the stand’s model. (2.4.0.132)
- Lightning spell and Zeus’s Wrath bolts: they dealt the Lightning Strike staff’s fixed impact damage (80 lightning, 400 at the very centre) whatever the staff; they now deal the spell’s damage (its share of the staff’s magic power) and apply its stun chance.

## 2.3.0 (in progress)

### Added
- **Character sheet**: the character tab no longer repeats the health, stamina and eitr bars or the food (already on the HUD and the food shortcuts), nor the active effects. It lists the character's cumulated bonuses instead, scrolling when long, in four sections (offense, defense, resources, other): the final values the game applies (health, stamina and eitr maximums with where they come from in a tooltip, armour, regenerations, fall damage, carry weight, movement speed, noise, stealth...), the final resistance to each damage type, every Effects.cfg effect and the level stats. Every line shows, at 0 when the character has none of it; effects carried by an item stay in its tooltip.
- **Split stack dialog**: the amount can be typed (Enter splits), with the stack size next to it; minimum |< and minus buttons left of the slider, plus and maximum >| right of it, as Auga diamond buttons. The dialog still opens at half the stack.
- **Crafting panel**: a minimum / minus / amount / plus / maximum selector (Auga diamond buttons) above the craft button picks how many to craft, from 1 up to what your resources allow (inventory and nearby chests, also limited by the room left in the inventory); it replaces Shift (×5) and is hidden for upgrades. A search field above the recipe list filters it by name as you type (player controls and hotkeys are blocked while typing, Tab, E and Escape no longer close the inventory). A column of diamond buttons on the window's left edge, notched into it, sorts the recipes: All, then swords, axes, maces, spears and atgeirs, knives and claws, bows, crossbows and the grappling hook, staffs (one and two-handed together), shields, armour (head, chest, legs and cape together), accessories, tools, ammunition, fishing bait and misc at gear stations; health, stamina, eitr and balanced food (eitr when it beats both other stats, otherwise the higher of health and stamina, balanced when they are equal; uncooked dishes by the food they bake into, raw ingredients for the cooking rack in misc; tankards and the drinking horn in misc); each button has the quantity buttons' diamond frame and its own white pictogram, the same at every station, and its tooltip names the filter at food stations; only the categories the station offers show. The recipe list no longer keeps empty space to scroll through when it is short, and goes back to the top when the station or filter changes. The upgrade tab and the repair button only show at stations that make upgradable or repairable gear (not the preparation table, cauldron, mead ketill...), the window then losing the repair button's notch. (2.3.0.391)
- **More location resets**: dvergr excavation camps, the petrified giants with their bone deposits, the dvergr harbour and lighthouse reset like the other camps and dungeons (after the reset delay following a first visit, or with o_resetdungeon). Dvergr towers (whole and ruined, outside dungeons) start their delay only once altered (a chest’s content changed, or a piece of the tower hit by a player), and come back as any of the tower versions, picked at random. They are added once to Dungeon.ResetLocations of existing configs; remove an ID there to keep that location as it is. (2.3.0.385, 390)
- **Quick repair**: the hammer's repair mends the piece aimed at and every damaged piece linked to it up to 3 pieces away (pieces touching it, then those touching them...), all in one go, for the cost of one repair; undamaged or unrepairable pieces are skipped. Hold the place button to repeat it as the aim moves (about three times a second). (376, 377)
- **Double doors**: opening or closing a door also opens or closes the identical door right beside it (same door, side by side in the same wall, facing the same way or mirrored) when it is in the same state; each door checks its own key and ward and swings away from the player. (375)
- **Building options**: an Auga window above the key hints (bottom right, over the rotation angles) while the hammer is in hand, showing the selected piece's options (or “No options for this piece”); its options are clicked with the build menu open (cursor shown). First option, for wood floors (wood and ashwood, 2x2, 1x1 and decorated): **Checkerboard floors** (off by default, kept in the local config): quick build lays every other floor turned 90°, ghosts included; quick removal also takes floors turned by quarter turns. (364, 365, 368)
- **Hidden creatures, hidden bars**: a creature's health bar no longer shows through terrain, rocks, trees or buildings; it appears again as soon as the creature can be seen (boss bars unchanged); each creature is checked about once a second, a few per frame, so crowds cost little. (351, 355, 356, 358)
- **Gear loot by biome**: every creature may drop a piece of gear of its biome (weapon, armour, shield or tool) with a random rarity, more often and rarer the higher its rank (normal 3%, captain 12%, elite 25%), the same odds in every biome. Biome bosses always drop a piece of the next biome with better rarities; the last boss, the best odds of all. Dungeon guardians drop no gear: their chest always holds one piece of the dungeon’s biome, with better odds than elites. Tamed creatures drop nothing. All odds in RaritySystem.cfg (BiomeLoot sections, added to existing files); gear listed in BiomeLootExceptions is never dropped this way (loot lists and the enchanting table still use it). Creatures with their own set (MobLoot sections: trolls, bears, abominations, cultists, Unbjorn, lox and now Asksvin with the Ask set) drop only that set, with the same odds as any creature of their rank (chance and rarities of its BiomeLoot section), and those sets never come from biome loot. Biome loot only drops gear the game crafts from raw materials: nothing without a recipe (the flaming sword, the starting wooden weapons...) and no upgraded variant made from another weapon (Nidhögg can drop, Nidhögg the Bleeding, Thundering or Primal cannot). (2.3.0.313, 324, 325, 326, 344)
- **Hugin’s tips on Overhaul**: the raven lands to tell, once, about levels and stat points, the dash, rarity, the enchanting table, catalysts, chests, moving a build, quick build, turning pieces and dungeons, each at its own moment (first experience, level 2, first weapon, first rare item...), with the keys as set; they join the compendium’s Hugin tab. In the compendium, a tip can show a short looping clip between its title and its text, staged in game on a flat checker map, with the keys pressed shown in the corner (Auga’s icons): quick build, turning pieces, moving a build, chests (taking all, renaming, private chests, trash), rarities (the ground auras, moving), catalysts then socketing them at the table, enchanting then modifying a line at the table (a tip with several clips shows one at a time, with an arrow on each side of it to go to the previous or next one); clicking a tip no longer scrolls the list back, levels and stat points, the dash (one jog step then the dash, with its pose and trails); window clips show the interface in English, one version for every language.
