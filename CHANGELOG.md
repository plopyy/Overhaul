# Changelog

## 2.3.0 (in progress)

### Added
- **Damage over time**, replacing the game's burning, poison, frost and lightning effects for players and creatures alike: fire, frost, lightning and poison hits put a Burn, Frostbite, Shock or Poison on the target (a fifth of the hit's raw element damage, at least 1, every second for 3 s), and Bleed will come from catalysts. A new one adds a stack (up to 5) and restarts its 3 s; each stack after the first adds 50 % damage; it ends at once when the time runs out. DoT damage never crits and goes through resistances; immunity prevents it. Side effects (normal / boss): Burn +50 % / +20 % chance to take a critical hit, Frostbite 50 % / 30 % slower, Shock stuns once every 10 s / 60 s, Poison deals 20 % / 10 % less damage, Bleed stops / halves health regeneration. New effects Burn, Frostbite, Shock, Poison and Bleed damage (+x %). Their icons and stacks show under the health bars, which now appear for every creature within 15 m without aiming at it, and in your own status effects. (2.3.0.123)
- **Enchanting table**, a new hammer piece (Crafting, at a workbench; provisional recipe: 10 stone, 6 fine wood, 4 bone fragments, 1 surtling core): Eikthyr’s altar holding maps of the cartography table, a rune table, crypt bones and eternal candles burning with their purple flames, all in the game’s own textures. It is a crafting station of its own (not a building point: no build range), built from stone, which can be moved like the other workbenches; its purple candle glow swells softly while you stand near it. Using it opens its window, beside your inventory; the item and trophy put on it stay on the table. **Enchant** tab: put a piece of gear and a creature trophy, see the odds of each rarity, then Enchant: the trophy is used up and the gear gets a new rarity (Uncommon to Mythic) with new enchantment lines, even if worse. The trophy's tier sets the odds: its creature's biome (Meadows tier 1, Black Forest 2...), one tier more for captains and elites, two for bosses (an Eikthyr trophy is worth a Swamp creature's); odds per tier in the EnchantTier sections of RaritySystem.cfg. **Modify** tab: pick a line and Reroll (5 bone fragments and 5 resin each time, taken from your inventory then the chests around the table; missing items blink red): the line offers itself and two other effects, the one clicked goes on the item and the offers close (closing the window keeps the current line). The first line rerolled becomes the only one that can be rerolled on that item, as many times as you like; enchanting the item again frees it. **Catalysts** tab: socket catalysts in the item's catalyst slots and take them out freely. (2.3.0.118)
- **Rarity system**, for weapons, armour, shields, tools and trinkets, driven by RaritySystem.cfg (the server’s file is sent to every player): rarities with their colour, aura, base stat bonus (a % of the damage, armour and block, upgrades included), empty enchantment lines and catalyst slots; catalyst items all have their own Catalyst rarity (candy pink, with a pink ground aura), never given to other gear, by loot or by enchanting (no enchantment lines or slots); only the gear listed in the Biome sections has a rarity (at least the first one), everything else is left out; creatures listed in the file drop pieces of their gear with a random rarity. Rarity shown in item slots (multicolour rarities as a 45° gradient, the first colour in the middle, and the item's catalyst slots as diamonds sitting on the left border line, centred on the height: empty, or holding the socketed catalyst's icon with its rarity's border), tooltips (item name, dividers and enchantment lines in the rarity colour, with its enchantment lines, empty ones as "Empty bonus +0", and catalyst slots) and as a ground aura. Admin test commands o_rarity <item> [rarity] and o_reroll [type] (new enchantments on the equipped item). Rarity names in RaritySystem.cfg are translation keys.
- **Effects and enchantments.** New Effects.cfg (sent by the server) lists every effect of the mod with its name, description, range and percent display: max health and eitr, health and eitr regen, eitr cost, food duration, added fire, frost, lightning, poison and spirit damage, attack, cast/draw and movement speed, fall damage and fall speed, extra projectiles, explosive projectiles, resistances to each element and physical damage type, parry, backstab and knockback bonuses, life steal, damage reflect, harvest and loot bonuses, carry weight, critical chance (Valheim's own critical hit on creatures: double damage with its sound and effect), physical damage, armour, mining and chopping damage, indestructible items (durability shown as ∞) and unlimited ammo for bows and crossbows (an arrow or bolt is still needed to choose what is fired, but it is never used up). Damage and armour enchantments raise the item's own stats, shown in its tooltip; Indestructible and explosive projectiles act on the item carrying them; explosive projectiles burst like the Staff of Embers fireball, at half its size and radius, dealing 20 fire damage around (and 20 chop and pickaxe damage, so trees, logs and rocks of the weapon's biome tier take it too). Gear now rolls real enchantments: the effects of Effects.cfg with an EnchantRarityMin and an EnchantBiomeValue, limited to the gear types of EnchantItemTypes (an item can get any enchantment of its rarity or a weaker one, never twice the same), worth the value of the item's biome (the new Biome sections list the gear of each biome). Tooltips show each enchantment with its effect name and value; equipped gear applies them. Empty enchantment lines (gear from before) are filled when the character loads or equips the item.
- **Mythic enchantments**: every Mythic item gets a special power (MythicEnchant sections of RaritySystem.cfg), shown under the catalyst slots with a star, its name in bold and its description in the mythic colours. First one, for bows and staffs: **Fan Volley**, 2 extra projectiles fired in a narrow fan with perfect accuracy (extra projectiles from other bonuses join the fan), with a crosshair mark for each projectile direction (enchantment lines and a 100 % leveling projectile chance included; the centre crosshair only shows for an odd count).  Second one, for staffs: **Zeus's Wrath**, each projectile has a 5 % chance to call a rain of 16 lightning bolts within 10 m over 6 seconds.
- Admin commands for the whole server, every player included: o_weather <weather>|reset sets the weather until the game's next weather change, then the weather goes on as usual (the game's env command does not work on a dedicated server); o_time hh:mm moves the time of day forward to the next hh:mm.

### Fixed
- Zeus’s Wrath: each projectile rolls its chance when it hits (one that vanishes without hitting anything never triggers it), and the rain of lightning falls around the triggering projectile’s impact point, whatever it hit, instead of sometimes on the caster, notably with the ice shard. Your own projectiles (spells, arrows, bolts…) now go through you instead of stopping or exploding on you.
- Valheim 1.0.20 compatibility: the SQLite world storage reads worlds of any format from 41 on (1.0.20 uses 42), and saves in the running game's format.
- The god command works on a dedicated server for an administrator with devcommands on.
- Armor stand window: the cells no longer show an extra helmet outline over every slot.
- A part of the mod that no longer fits the game (for example after a Valheim update) is now skipped and reported in the log, instead of stopping every Overhaul feature loaded after it.
- The empty inventory windows no longer flash on screen for a moment before the inventory slides in, the first time it is opened.
- No more burst of 32 "Destroying components immediately" errors in the log when the inventory is first opened (the leveling stat cards removed the tooltips of their - and + buttons at the wrong moment).

### Changed
- The small Ashlands ruins (AshlandRuins) are no longer reset like dungeons: a base built in them keeps its ruin as it was. The guarded Ashlands fortresses (with ballistas) and the goblin villages are still reset.
- Settings: the "Mods" tab is now called **Overhaul** and has sub-tabs like the Controls tab: **Shortcuts** (every Overhaul shortcut) and **Gameplay** (the Dash option).
- Lightning Strike staff: the lightning now falls at once exactly where the crosshair points (one for each projectile the staff would fire: extra ones spread around the crosshair, in a fan with Fan Volley), instead of after a slow projectile, and the whole lightning is white with a cyan glow instead of red; its blocking shield is the lightning one instead of the blood one.
- Battleaxes chop trees better: 15 more chopping damage than the one-handed axe of their tier, +3 per upgrade level like it (black metal battleaxe: 75 to 84). The crystal battleaxe sits between iron and black metal (70 to 79).
- The equipped weapon or tool is also marked by a pure white slot border in the inventory, like in the hotbar.
- Dvergr circlets: they no longer thin fog, smoke and dust (the Dvergr circlet is back to its light only). The spirit circlet now only thins the Mistlands mist around you (10 % opacity up to twice a wisp’s clearing radius, back to full at three times); the rest of the world keeps its fog and smoke; the mist particles keep their own opacity, the huge mist particles that wrap the wearer are thinned too, and the mist's slow breathing now only varies between 20 and 30 % of its opacity.

## 2.2.2 (in progress)

### Fixed
- At startup, the crypt rooms looked through for the boss rooms' materials were let go while their textures were still loading: the game closed their files mid-load ("failed to load runetablet_vegvisir" in the console) and a vegvisir could show without its textures. They are now let go once loaded. (2.3.0.102)
- Tooltips (inventory items, skills, leveling stats and every other element with a tooltip) often did not show: one closed as soon as the pointer moved over another part of the element (its icon, its text…) and stayed closed until the pointer left the element and came back. A tooltip now covers its whole element, and reopens after the usual short delay while the pointer rests on it. (2.2.2.1)
- Character selection screen: with a mod that turns the game's achievements back on, the character list stopped with an error (NullReferenceException in GetActiveCombatDifficulty) while showing each character's deaths, builds and crafts. (2.2.2.1)

## 2.2.1 (released)

### Added
- **Epic Loot compatibility.** Epic Loot's own item display, which broke the Auga interface, is replaced by Overhaul's: Auga tooltips show the rarity, the magic effects, shard slots and legendary/mythic set bonuses like the other stats, with shorter French effect names ("Vitesse d'attaque +10%" instead of "Augmente la vitesse d'attaque de 10%") taken from Overhaul's translation file. Item tooltips are reorganised to make room (wider, more compact, damage shown as the actual range with the elemental stat included, staff shield instead of blocking stats, remaining stats such as the chance to apply an effect shown with their value on the right) and scaled down if still taller than the screen. Status effect names and descriptions in tooltips are translated again. (2.2.1.59)
- Server option `[Items] StackSize100`, on by default: turn it off to keep the vanilla stack sizes instead of stacks of 100. It applies right away, even when changed during a game. (2.2.1.18)
  With the option off, stacks bigger than the vanilla limit in your inventory, or taken from a chest, are split into normal stacks (100 wood becomes 2 × 50); what does not fit is dropped at your feet. (2.2.1.19)
- Settings > Mods: **Dash (replaces sprint)** option, on by default. When turned off, the Run key sprints like in vanilla Valheim and drains stamina; sprint speed is scaled to Overhaul's faster jog, so sprinting stays faster than jogging. Per-player setting. (2.2.1.1)
- Settings > Mods: the **Take all** key can be changed (printed R key by default). Shift + this key still renames a chest and swaps gear with an armor stand, and on-screen hints show the chosen key. (2.2.1.3)

### Changed
- New item slot design (Compatible with Epic Loot rarities). (2.2.1.102)
- Trinkets give their bonus permanently while worn, like any other equipment, instead of only for a few seconds once the adrenaline bar is full. Adrenaline is disabled for now: no more adrenaline bar (kept enabled when Epic Loot is installed, for its adrenaline enchantments). (2.2.1.25)
- Station upgrades (workbench, forge…) can be placed or moved right next to each other: the "too close to another upgrade" limit is removed. They must still be within reach of their station. (2.2.1.17)
- Oven: each of the 4 slots takes a stack of one raw dish. The first one cooks in its place in the oven, is ejected in front of it once cooked (like other production machines, never despawning), then the next one starts. Cooked dishes left in the oven by earlier versions are ejected too. (2.2.1.7)
- Items dropped from the inventory fly towards the crosshair instead of the character's facing, keeping the vanilla arc and speed. (2.2.1.4)

### Fixed
- Since Valheim 1.0.17, the end-game credits could stay open invisibly in the background and grey out other windows (like portal window). They now stay closed unless the end credits are actually playing. (2.2.1.6)
- Dedicated server: a dungeon whose reset was refused (for example because it touches another tracked location) was retried every 5 minutes, regenerating a whole dungeon and its terrain each time. This could freeze the server for several minutes, disconnect players and block shutdown. A refused reset now waits 6 hours before the next attempt, and the log names the object that blocks it. (2.2.1.10)
- Dungeon and camp resets are no longer refused because of what is on the surface around them: objects of a neighbouring location are left in place, and a camp whose old layout differs from the reference is still reset. Only a player inside a dungeon can postpone a reset. (2.2.1.11)
- Surface locations (camps, ruins, Mistlands entrances…) no longer wait for nearby players before resetting: players standing in the reset area are moved just outside it, then the reset goes ahead. (2.2.1.13)
- Dungeon interiors can no longer overlap: every generated dungeon interior (Hildir's crypt and cave, Mörkhalla, the Ashlands tunnels…), not only those with an Overhaul boss room, moves at its next reset to its own free height lane high above the world. The entrance and exit doors, the interior lighting and weather follow it. A dungeon whose rooms do not fit in a lane keeps its original height. (2.2.1.16)
