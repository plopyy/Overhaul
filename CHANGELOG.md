# Changelog

## 2.5.0 (in progress)

## 2.4.0 (in progress)

### Added
- **Grimoire stand**, a new hammer piece (Crafting, at a workbench: 8 fine wood, 4 bronze, 1 surtling core): a candlelit book stand and its tall candle holder in the game’s own wood wall material, wearing, weathering and lighting like any wooden wall (the grain running along their posts), with candles burning with their own flames (no black wicks) and a soft, warm yellow light that flickers. Its grimoire is closed, plays its opening animation as soon as any player comes near (within 4 m) and its closing animation when nobody is left around; every player sees it. As the book opens, the three candles of the tall holder rise about 15 cm off it, one after the other, and float with a gentle up-and-down sway as if stirred by the wind (each at its own rhythm, in a seamless loop) while a player stays near; as the book closes they settle back exactly where they were. Using it opens the grimoire window, which closes if you walk away from the stand. It can be moved with the move key like the other functional pieces. Its build menu icon shows the stand itself. (2.4.0.105)
- **Eitr from food**, set in the new **Foods.cfg** (sent by the server): every food without eitr of its own now gives 15 % of its health + stamina as eitr (carrot soup: 9, bread: 14), and four new **eitr dishes** at the cauldron lead up to the Mistlands’ eitr dishes (stuffed mushroom: 75 eitr): **Yellow mushroom stew** (Black Forest: yellow mushrooms, mushrooms, thistle; 15 eitr), **Turnip and mushroom soup** (Swamp: turnips, yellow mushrooms, bloodbag; 30 eitr), **Onion infusion** (Mountains: onions, mushrooms, honey; 45 eitr) and **Cloud flatbread** (Plains: barley flour, cloudberries, yellow mushrooms; 60 eitr), each with low health and stamina and an eitr-blue icon.
- **Overhaul staffs**, one per biome, new items next to the game’s staffs (left unchanged), each crafted from a wood, the biome’s metal and a biome ingredient: **Sylvan staff** (Black Forest, forge: wood, bronze, surtling cores), **Spectral staff** (Swamp, forge: ancient bark, iron, ectoplasm), **Runic staff** (Mountains, forge: core wood, silver, freeze glands), **Totemic staff** (Plains, forge: fine wood, black metal, fuling totems), **Mist staff** (Mistlands, galdr table: Yggdrasil wood, black metal, black cores) and **Ash staff** (Ashlands, galdr table: blackwood, flametal, molten cores). The Totemic staff has its own model (wood with its own texture, black metal rings, a fuling totem with its purple glow and sparks) and its own icon, drawn by the game from the model like the game’s staff icons (head in close-up, staff on a diagonal, its head glow included); the **Spectral staff** takes the staff of fracturing’s model with its smooth crystals and gems turned from red to the ectoplasm’s pale aqua green, and the fuling totem’s glow and sparks of the Totemic staff around its head, in that same colour (and its own icon too); the **Mist staff** has its own model too, the staff of embers’ wood in a darker, warmer brown, with black metal bands and a black core hanging under its crook, with the fuling totem’s purple glow and sparks around the core (and its own icon); the Ash staff uses the staff of embers’ model until its own is made. Each has its own blocking shield and follows the rarity system. Staffs no longer have blunt or elemental damage but a new base stat, **Magic power** (20 for the Sylvan staff up to 100 for the Ash staff, raised by each upgrade level and by rarity), shown in the tooltip; magic power has no element: each spell deals a percent of it as its own element’s damage (a 120 % fire spell with 100 power = 120 fire damage), shown in the grimoire; until the grimoire spells are cast, the staffs fire their base staff’s spell at 100 % (fireball: fire, ice shards: frost). New **Magic power** effect in Effects.cfg, flat, added to the equipped staff’s magic power: a gear enchantment for now, and the one effect class skills and buffs will use too. Magic power is the staff’s own damage stat, computed like any weapon damage (upgrade levels, rarity, damage effects, skill), and only that part of the hit is turned into the spell’s element when the spell hits (a weapon’s other damage, blunt, lightning…, is dealt as usual); in the tooltip it is the Magic power line next to the other damage lines. **Magic** is a damage category like Physical: magic power, fire, frost and lightning. The game’s generic damage is now magic power on every weapon: it only serves spells and is never dealt by a hit without a spell. New effects: **Magic damage** (%, staff enchantment, raises magic power, fire, frost and lightning damage) and **Magic resistance** (%, armour and shield enchantment, less fire, frost and lightning damage taken). Each staff holds 4 spell slots (2 normal spells, 1 special, 1 shield): the grimoire now installs spells in the equipped staff, and each staff keeps its own.
- **First spells**, known from the start and listed in the new **Spells.cfg** (sent by the server; name, element, type, tier, unlock level, damage in % of the magic power, eitr cost, cast attack and projectile, projectile size, speed and gravity, slow and stun values): **Fireball** (fire, 100 %, 5 eitr: Overhaul’s straight fireball, 50 % faster, exploding on impact and setting enemies on fire), **Ice shard** (ice, 100 %, 5 eitr: one ice shard twice as big, particles and glow included, 50 % faster, flying straight at the crosshair without falling, slowing the movement and attacks of the creatures hit by 50 % for 4 s) and **Lightning** (lightning, 100 %, 5 eitr: Overhaul’s lightning falling where you aim, 35 % chance to stun each creature hit for 2 s: no move, no attack). Each has its own icon (a fireball, an ice shard, a lightning bolt), and the grimoire's element tabs and page header have their own emblems (a flame, an ice crystal, a bolt) instead of the game's icons; each element has its colour: orange-red fire, blue ice, yellow lightning. Bookmark icons keep the same gap to the bookmark's end whether it is open or not, and the installed spell slots are named Normal, Normal, Special and Shield, an empty slot shows no text. The grimoire columns are tiers (Tier I to III); the rank pips under a spell are the spell’s own rank. An Overhaul staff casts the spell installed in its first slot with the light attack and the one of its second slot with the heavy attack; with no spell installed it does not attack.
- **Grimoire** (spell book), first version, opened with F1 for now (Settings > Overhaul > Shortcuts): an open book in the Auga look. On the right page, one bookmark per element (fire, ice, lightning) and the element's spells by type (normal, special, shield) and rank, ranks II and III showing the class level that unlocks them. On the left page, the details of the clicked spell (eitr, damage, cast time, area) and the 4 installed spells (2 normal spells, 1 special, 1 shield). Installing a spell: drag and drop it onto a matching slot. The spells are those of Spells.cfg (the demo list is gone), empty cards are hidden; the installed spells are those of the equipped staff.

### Fixed
- Grimoire stand: it could show the pack’s own wood instead of the game’s wood wall planks; its wood is now the wood wall’s material itself, set in the stand’s model. (2.4.0.132)
- Lightning spell and Zeus’s Wrath bolts: they dealt the Lightning Strike staff’s fixed impact damage (80 lightning, 400 at the very centre) whatever the staff; they now deal the spell’s damage (its share of the staff’s magic power) and apply its stun chance.

## 2.3.0 (in progress)

### Added
- **Dragon Fist**, the mythic enchantment of fist weapons (claws, knucklechains): every 4th attack hurls a dragon of energy straight ahead (Hovl Studio’s Dragon punch effect, seen by every player) that deals 100 % (Meadows) to 170 % (Deep North) of the attack’s damage to every enemy in its path up to 5 m, once each. New **Fists** gear category for enchantments and mythics (EnchantItemTypes / ItemTypes). (2.3.0.150)
- **Wind Blade**, the mythic enchantment of two-handed swords: the last hit of each combo sends a translucent steel-blue crescent wave straight ahead, 8 m in 0.4 s, that deals 60 % (Meadows) to 130 % (Deep North) of the attack’s damage to every enemy it crosses, once each; seen by every player. New **Greatsword** gear category. (2.3.0.151)
- **Blade Nova**, the mythic enchantment of knives: each attack has a 10 % (Meadows) to 18 % (Deep North) chance to send 10 spectral daggers flying out from a perfect circle around you, to 6 m; every enemy they reach takes 60 % of the attack’s damage as pierce damage, once; seen by every player. New **Dagger** gear category. (2.3.0.152)
- **Earthquake**, the mythic enchantment of two-handed maces (sledges): 1 s after each ground hit, a second, wider shockwave (dust ring, debris and a warm flash) bursts where it struck, dealing 50 % (Meadows) to 120 % (Deep North) of the attack’s damage to every enemy within 6 m and staggering them; seen by every player. (2.3.0.155)
- Gear categories for each kind of melee weapon, usable in EnchantItemTypes (Effects.cfg) and in the mythics’ ItemTypes: Sword, Greatsword, OneHandedAxe, Battleaxe (dual axes included), Mace, Sledge, Dagger, Atgeir, Spear, Fists. (2.3.0.154)
- **Catalyst effects**: every catalyst socketed in worn gear acts, once whatever the gear (the same catalyst cannot be socketed twice in an item), with fixed values set in its RaritySystem.cfg section and shown in the tooltips. Amber pearl +10 % damage on a creature at full health; Hard antler a 10 lightning bolt every 4 attacks; Ruby 5 % life steal under 30 % health; Surtling core 10 fire damage to melee attackers; Ectoplasm cancels the next hit under 25 % health (every 120 s); Writhing roots 15 % chance to root the target for 3 s (Rooted: it cannot move but still attacks; its own icon); Crystal sends back 50 % of a parried hit; Freeze gland frost immunity and +10 % speed in the Mountains and Deep North; Dragon tear +25 % damage on a creature under 30 % health; Yagluth's drop a fire burst on each kill; Eitr 15 % chance per spell to refill all eitr; Black core +5 % damage per kill for 10 s (up to 3); Celestial feather 25 % higher jumps; Molten core fire immunity and no lava or hot water damage; red, green, blue, orange and yellow gems Bleed, Poison, Frostbite, Burn or Shock every 3 attacks (hit or not) and +25 % to that DoT. New orange (emerald cut) and yellow (pear cut) gems, made like the game's own and found wherever the red gem is, with the same chance. Older configuration files get the catalyst effects and the new gems added. (2.3.0.134)
- **Damage over time**, replacing every damage over time of the game (burning, poison, frost, lightning, spirit, including attacks that carried them) for players and creatures alike: fire, frost, lightning and poison hits (fires and campfires included) put a Burn, Frostbite, Shock or Poison on the target (a fifth of the hit's raw element damage, at least 1, every second for 3 s), and Bleed will come from catalysts. A new one adds a stack (up to 5) and restarts its 3 s; each stack after the first adds 50 % damage; it ends at once when the time runs out. DoT damage never crits and goes through resistances; immunity prevents it. Side effects (normal / boss): Burn +50 % / +20 % chance to take a critical hit, Frostbite 50 % / 30 % slower, Shock stuns once every 10 s / 60 s, Poison deals 20 % / 10 % less damage, Bleed stops / halves health regeneration. New effects Burn, Frostbite, Shock, Poison and Bleed damage (+x %). Their icons and stacks show under the health bars, which now appear for every creature within 50 m without aiming at it, and in your own status effects. Ashlands lava burns with a Burn (a fifth of its damage). New on/off effects Fire immunity (no fire damage, no Burn) and Frost immunity (no frost damage, no Frostbite, no cold or freezing from the weather). (2.3.0.125)
- **Enchanting table**, a new hammer piece (Crafting, at a workbench; provisional recipe: 10 stone, 6 fine wood, 4 bone fragments, 1 surtling core): Eikthyr’s altar holding maps of the cartography table, a rune table, crypt bones and eternal candles burning with their purple flames, all in the game’s own textures. It is a crafting station of its own (not a building point: no build range), built from stone, which can be moved like the other workbenches; its purple candle glow swells softly while you stand near it, and its six wooden runes rise about 15 cm off the table one after the other and float with a gentle up-and-down sway, as if stirred by the wind (each at its own rhythm, in a seamless loop), their violet runes glowing brighter and pulsing softly with a faint violet light cast on the table, while any player is near (within 5 m), settling back exactly where they were when nobody is left around. Using it opens its window, beside your inventory; the item and trophy put on it stay on the table. **Enchant** tab: put a piece of gear and a creature trophy, see the odds of each rarity, then Enchant: the trophy is used up and the gear gets a new rarity (Uncommon to Mythic) with new enchantment lines, even if worse. The trophy's tier sets the odds: its creature's biome (Meadows tier 1, Black Forest 2...), one tier more for captains and elites, two for bosses (an Eikthyr trophy is worth a Swamp creature's); odds per tier in the EnchantTier sections of RaritySystem.cfg. **Modify** tab: pick a line and Reroll (5 bone fragments and 5 resin each time, taken from your inventory then the chests around the table; missing items blink red): the line offers itself and two other effects, the one clicked goes on the item and the offers close (closing the window keeps the current line). The first line rerolled becomes the only one that can be rerolled on that item, as many times as you like; enchanting the item again frees it. **Catalysts** tab: socket catalysts in the item's catalyst slots and take them out freely. (2.3.0.118)
- **Rarity system**, for weapons, armour, shields, tools and trinkets, driven by RaritySystem.cfg (the server’s file is sent to every player): rarities with their colour, aura, base stat bonus (a % of the damage, armour and block, upgrades included), empty enchantment lines and catalyst slots; catalyst items all have their own Catalyst rarity (candy pink, with a pink ground aura), never given to other gear, by loot or by enchanting (no enchantment lines or slots); only the gear listed in the Biome sections has a rarity (at least the first one), everything else is left out; creatures listed in the file drop pieces of their gear with a random rarity. Rarity shown in item slots (multicolour rarities as a 45° gradient, the first colour in the middle, and the item's catalyst slots as diamonds sitting on the left border line, centred on the height: empty, or holding the socketed catalyst's icon with its rarity's border), tooltips (item name, dividers and enchantment lines in the rarity colour, with its enchantment lines, empty ones as "Empty bonus +0", and catalyst slots) and as a ground aura. Admin test commands o_rarity <item> [rarity] and o_reroll [type] (new enchantments on the equipped item). Rarity names in RaritySystem.cfg are translation keys.
- **Effects and enchantments.** New Effects.cfg (sent by the server) lists every effect of the mod with its name, description, range and percent display: max health and eitr, health and eitr regen, eitr cost, food duration, added fire, frost, lightning, poison and spirit damage, attack, cast/draw and movement speed, fall damage and fall speed, extra projectiles, explosive projectiles, resistances to each element and physical damage type, parry, backstab and knockback bonuses, life steal, damage reflect, harvest and loot bonuses, carry weight, critical chance (Valheim's own critical hit on creatures: double damage with its sound and effect), physical damage, armour, mining and chopping damage, indestructible items (durability shown as ∞) and unlimited ammo for bows and crossbows (an arrow or bolt is still needed to choose what is fired, but it is never used up). Damage and armour enchantments raise the item's own stats, shown in its tooltip; Indestructible and explosive projectiles act on the item carrying them; explosive projectiles burst like the Staff of Embers fireball, at half its size and radius, dealing 20 fire damage around (and 20 chop and pickaxe damage, so trees, logs and rocks of the weapon's biome tier take it too). Gear now rolls real enchantments: the effects of Effects.cfg with an EnchantRarityMin and an EnchantBiomeValue, limited to the gear types of EnchantItemTypes (an item can get any enchantment of its rarity or a weaker one, never twice the same), worth the value of the item's biome (the new Biome sections list the gear of each biome). Tooltips show each enchantment with its effect name and value; equipped gear applies them. Empty enchantment lines (gear from before) are filled when the character loads or equips the item.
- **Mythic enchantments**: every Mythic item gets a special power (MythicEnchant sections of RaritySystem.cfg), shown under the catalyst slots with a star, its name in bold and its description in the mythic colours. First one, for bows and staffs: **Fan Volley**, 2 extra projectiles fired in a narrow fan with perfect accuracy (extra projectiles from other bonuses join the fan), with a crosshair mark for each projectile direction (enchantment lines and a 100 % leveling projectile chance included; the centre crosshair only shows for an odd count).  Second one, for staffs: **Zeus's Wrath**, each projectile has a 5 % chance to call a rain of 16 lightning bolts within 10 m over 6 seconds.
- Admin commands for the whole server, every player included: o_weather <weather>|reset sets the weather until the game's next weather change, then the weather goes on as usual (the game's env command does not work on a dedicated server); o_time hh:mm moves the time of day forward to the next hh:mm.

### Changed
- Enchanting table: its parts now wear the game’s own materials straight from its model (altar, bones, candles, maps, runes), with no material swapped while the game loads. (2.3.0.149)
- One item slot for every place: the hotbar and quick slot cells are now the inventory's own slot in its HUD look (a prefab variant), so everything on it (rarity border, catalyst diamonds, quality badge...) shows in the hotbar too, and any later change applies everywhere. Its two parameters: IsHUD (on: transparent black ground, no rarity ground; off: the window's brown ground, darker to the left, with the rarity ground) and HotKey (the key shown in the top left corner, none when empty). (2.3.0.146)
- Creature health bars look like your own health bar, but straight: black frame, red bar over a white slow part (green for tamed creatures), with a tick every 25 % of the bar so every creature's bar keeps the same size; the level stars sit on its top edge. They show for every creature within 50 m (was 15 m), aimed at or not. (2.3.0.141)

### Fixed
- A server shutting down (restart, update) now disconnects its players first, before saving the world: they go back to the main menu with the disconnected message, instead of staying in game with the connection problem icon while the server was already restarting. (2.3.0.144)
- Item slots: the catalyst diamonds are 50 % bigger (they may spill over the next cell), to be seen clearly. (2.3.0.144)
- Enchanting table: a catalyst slot takes a single catalyst, not a stack. (2.3.0.143)
- Character selection screen: with achievements turned back on, the stats could still stop the character list with an error when the game's server options window existed but was not set up yet; they now fall back to the default difficulty in every case (from 2.2.2.3). (2.3.0.140)
- With a full inventory, items on the ground are no longer pulled to your feet without being picked up ("Your inventory is full"), empty quick slots included: they only count as room for the items they accept. (2.3.0.142)
- An older Effects.cfg (a server's, sent to every player) gets the effects added since, notably the DoT bonuses and the Fire and Frost immunities: without them the Molten core and Freeze gland immunities did nothing and DoTs still landed. (2.3.0.138)
- Damage over time icons now show under creature health bars and in your own status effects (the effects list included): the Overhaul health bars no longer depend on the (disabled by default) Hud UI module, they have their own EnemyHud module, on by default, and their own canvas (they were built but never drawn). (2.3.0.137)
- Zeus’s Wrath: each projectile rolls its chance when it hits (one that vanishes without hitting anything never triggers it), and the rain of lightning falls around the triggering projectile’s impact point, whatever it hit, instead of sometimes on the caster, notably with the ice shard. Your own projectiles (spells, arrows, bolts…) now go through you instead of stopping or exploding on you.
- Valheim 1.0.20 compatibility: the SQLite world storage reads worlds of any format from 41 on (1.0.20 uses 42), and saves in the running game's format.
- The god command works on a dedicated server for an administrator with devcommands on.
- Armor stand window: the cells no longer show an extra helmet outline over every slot.
- A part of the mod that no longer fits the game (for example after a Valheim update) is now skipped and reported in the log, instead of stopping every Overhaul feature loaded after it.
- The empty inventory windows no longer flash on screen for a moment before the inventory slides in, the first time it is opened.
- No more burst of 32 "Destroying components immediately" errors in the log when the inventory is first opened (the leveling stat cards removed the tooltips of their - and + buttons at the wrong moment).

### Changed
- The small Ashlands ruins (AshlandRuins) are no longer reset like dungeons: a base built in them keeps its ruin as it was. The guarded Ashlands fortresses (with ballistas) and the goblin villages are still reset.
- Settings: the "Mods" tab is now called **Overhaul** and has sub-tabs like the Controls tab: **Shortcuts** (every Overhaul shortcut) and **Gameplay** (the Dash option).
- Lightning Strike staff: the lightning now falls at once exactly where the crosshair points (one for each projectile the staff would fire: extra ones spread around the crosshair, in a fan with Fan Volley), instead of after a slow projectile, and the whole lightning is white with a cyan glow instead of red; its blocking shield is the lightning one instead of the blood one.
- Battleaxes chop trees better: 15 more chopping damage than the one-handed axe of their tier, +3 per upgrade level like it (black metal battleaxe: 75 to 84). The crystal battleaxe sits between iron and black metal (70 to 79).
- The equipped weapon or tool is also marked by a pure white slot border in the inventory, like in the hotbar.
- Dvergr circlets: they no longer thin fog, smoke and dust (the Dvergr circlet is back to its light only). The spirit circlet now only thins the Mistlands mist around you (10 % opacity up to twice a wisp’s clearing radius, back to full at three times); the rest of the world keeps its fog and smoke; the mist particles keep their own opacity, the huge mist particles that wrap the wearer are thinned too, and the mist's slow breathing now only varies between 20 and 30 % of its opacity.

## 2.2.2 (in progress)

### Fixed
- At startup, the crypt rooms looked through for the boss rooms' materials were let go while their textures were still loading: the game closed their files mid-load ("failed to load runetablet_vegvisir" in the console) and a vegvisir could show without its textures. They are now let go once loaded. (2.3.0.102)
- Tooltips (inventory items, skills, leveling stats and every other element with a tooltip) often did not show: one closed as soon as the pointer moved over another part of the element (its icon, its text…) and stayed closed until the pointer left the element and came back. A tooltip now covers its whole element, and reopens after the usual short delay while the pointer rests on it. (2.2.2.1)
- Character selection screen: with a mod that turns the game's achievements back on, the character list stopped with an error (NullReferenceException in GetActiveCombatDifficulty) while showing each character's deaths, builds and crafts. (2.2.2.1)

## 2.2.1 (released)

### Added
- **Epic Loot compatibility.** Epic Loot's own item display, which broke the Auga interface, is replaced by Overhaul's: Auga tooltips show the rarity, the magic effects, shard slots and legendary/mythic set bonuses like the other stats, with shorter French effect names ("Vitesse d'attaque +10%" instead of "Augmente la vitesse d'attaque de 10%") taken from Overhaul's translation file. Item tooltips are reorganised to make room (wider, more compact, damage shown as the actual range with the elemental stat included, staff shield instead of blocking stats, remaining stats such as the chance to apply an effect shown with their value on the right) and scaled down if still taller than the screen. Status effect names and descriptions in tooltips are translated again. (2.2.1.59)
- Server option `[Items] StackSize100`, on by default: turn it off to keep the vanilla stack sizes instead of stacks of 100. It applies right away, even when changed during a game. (2.2.1.18)
  With the option off, stacks bigger than the vanilla limit in your inventory, or taken from a chest, are split into normal stacks (100 wood becomes 2 × 50); what does not fit is dropped at your feet. (2.2.1.19)
- Settings > Mods: **Dash (replaces sprint)** option, on by default. When turned off, the Run key sprints like in vanilla Valheim and drains stamina; sprint speed is scaled to Overhaul's faster jog, so sprinting stays faster than jogging. Per-player setting. (2.2.1.1)
- Settings > Mods: the **Take all** key can be changed (printed R key by default). Shift + this key still renames a chest and swaps gear with an armor stand, and on-screen hints show the chosen key. (2.2.1.3)

### Changed
- New item slot design (Compatible with Epic Loot rarities). (2.2.1.102)
- Trinkets give their bonus permanently while worn, like any other equipment, instead of only for a few seconds once the adrenaline bar is full. Adrenaline is disabled for now: no more adrenaline bar (kept enabled when Epic Loot is installed, for its adrenaline enchantments). (2.2.1.25)
- Station upgrades (workbench, forge…) can be placed or moved right next to each other: the "too close to another upgrade" limit is removed. They must still be within reach of their station. (2.2.1.17)
- Oven: each of the 4 slots takes a stack of one raw dish. The first one cooks in its place in the oven, is ejected in front of it once cooked (like other production machines, never despawning), then the next one starts. Cooked dishes left in the oven by earlier versions are ejected too. (2.2.1.7)
- Items dropped from the inventory fly towards the crosshair instead of the character's facing, keeping the vanilla arc and speed. (2.2.1.4)

### Fixed
- Since Valheim 1.0.17, the end-game credits could stay open invisibly in the background and grey out other windows (like portal window). They now stay closed unless the end credits are actually playing. (2.2.1.6)
- Dedicated server: a dungeon whose reset was refused (for example because it touches another tracked location) was retried every 5 minutes, regenerating a whole dungeon and its terrain each time. This could freeze the server for several minutes, disconnect players and block shutdown. A refused reset now waits 6 hours before the next attempt, and the log names the object that blocks it. (2.2.1.10)
- Dungeon and camp resets are no longer refused because of what is on the surface around them: objects of a neighbouring location are left in place, and a camp whose old layout differs from the reference is still reset. Only a player inside a dungeon can postpone a reset. (2.2.1.11)
- Surface locations (camps, ruins, Mistlands entrances…) no longer wait for nearby players before resetting: players standing in the reset area are moved just outside it, then the reset goes ahead. (2.2.1.13)
- Dungeon interiors can no longer overlap: every generated dungeon interior (Hildir's crypt and cave, Mörkhalla, the Ashlands tunnels…), not only those with an Overhaul boss room, moves at its next reset to its own free height lane high above the world. The entrance and exit doors, the interior lighting and weather follow it. A dungeon whose rooms do not fit in a lane keeps its original height. (2.2.1.16)
